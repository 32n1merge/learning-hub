Your Colorado instruction permit plan

The written knowledge exam tests the Colorado Driver Handbook, not clever guessing. Start with the legal pathway, then treat every drive with a permit as deliberate practice.

Permit basics: bring the required identity, residency, and lawful-presence documents; pass vision and knowledge tests; and, if under 18, bring the required parent/guardian authorization. DMV requirements and fees can change—confirm the current checklist and make an appointment at Colorado DMV Teen Drivers before you go.

Practice toward a license

For a minor license, Colorado commonly requires holding the permit at least 12 months and documenting 50 hours of supervised driving, including 10 at night. A permit is not a license: drive only with a qualified supervising driver as the handbook requires, and follow all teen-passenger and phone restrictions.

Exam habit: when a question uses “must,” “always,” or “unless,” slow down. It is usually testing a specific rule or exception—not your general sense of what seems polite.
