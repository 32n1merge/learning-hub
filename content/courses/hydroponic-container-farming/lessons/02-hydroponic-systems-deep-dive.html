🔧 Hydroponic Systems Deep Dive

Not all hydroponic systems are created equal. Each design tackles the same problem — getting oxygenated nutrient solution to plant roots — in a different way. Choosing the right system for your crops is the single most important design decision you'll make.

🎯 For a container vegetable farm: Most commercial container farms use a hybrid approach — NFT channels for leafy greens and herbs stacked vertically on the walls, with Dutch bucket (drip) systems for large fruiting crops like tomatoes and peppers along the center aisle. This maximizes both space and crop diversity.

Why NFT + Dutch Buckets Is the Container Sweet Spot

A 40-foot container gives you about 320 square feet of floor space, but with vertical NFT channels on both walls (4–5 tiers), you get 800–1,200 square feet of growing area. Here's the layout strategy:

  • Wall channels (NFT): Fast-growing leafy greens — lettuce (30-day cycle), basil, kale, spinach, arugula. These are your high-turnover cash crops.
  • Floor / center aisle (Dutch buckets): Fruiting crops — tomatoes, peppers, cucumbers, eggplant. These need more root space and structural support.
  • Nursery corner: A dedicated propagation area with seedling trays and a small DWC tub for starting new plants.
