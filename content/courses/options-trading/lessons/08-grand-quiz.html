Community & Continuing Education

This course gave you the foundation. Real wisdom comes from practice and community:

  • r/thetagang — For premium sellers. The anti-WSB, focused on consistent income.

  • Paper Trading — Every major broker (Thinkorswim, Tastyworks, Webull) offers paper trading. Use it. Test strategies risk-free for 3-6 months before deploying real capital.
Final Word: Options are the most powerful retail trading instruments ever created. They can hedge risk, generate income, and produce life-changing returns. They can also destroy accounts, marriages, and mental health. The difference isn't intelligence — it's discipline, risk management, and the humility to know that nobody beats the market every time. Position size is everything. Survive first. Profit second.
